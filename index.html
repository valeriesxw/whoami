About me

My passion for technology grew at PulseSync, where I worked alongside developers and cross-functional teams on AI-powered applications and smart fitness technology — and realised I wanted to be building the infrastructure, not just coordinating around it.

That realisation led me to Generation Singapore's Cloud Support & DevOps Bootcamp, an intensive full-time programme covering Linux administration, networking, Bash scripting, Python, Docker, Git and cloud troubleshooting.

Before that pivot, I spent over eight years delivering cross-functional projects: coordinating international teams at Paris Saint-Germain APAC, running programmes and marketing at WWTBF (The Cage) in sports technology, and supporting logistics at Decathlon Singapore. That background means I already know how to work with engineers, ask the right questions, document decisions clearly, and keep a project moving — I'm now applying it to cloud and DevOps work.

I'm looking for a role where I can keep building hands-on technical skill inside a collaborative engineering team.
